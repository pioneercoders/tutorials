### What is generative AI?
Generative AI refers to a type of artificial intelligence that can create new content—such as text, images, music, audio, video, and code—by learning patterns from existing data. Instead of just analyzing or categorizing data, generative AI models generate outputs that resemble human-created content.


### 🧠 What Makes Generative AI Special?  
At its core, generative AI uses machine learning, particularly deep learning and neural networks, to mimic how humans produce content. The most common architectures include:  

Transformer models (e.g., GPT, BERT, T5)  

GANs (Generative Adversarial Networks) for images and video  

VAEs (Variational Autoencoders)
